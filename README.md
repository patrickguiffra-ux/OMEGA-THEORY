## Abstract

Omega is a cosmological and gravitational framework in which the fundamental entity is not spacetime but a condensate that is continuously created. Spacetime, general relativity (GR) and relative time appear only locally, as properties of the perturbations of this medium. The universe has no age and no Big Bang: creation is eternal and proceeds in distinct episodes. No dark-matter particle and no dark-energy fluid are introduced.

The framework reproduces the galaxy data with a0 = 1.16 × 10⁻¹⁰ m s⁻², the high-redshift rotation curves (which exclude a0 ∝ H(z) at Δχ² = +83), the expansion history, the one-dimensional Lyman-α power measured by DESI, and the black-body CMB and its acoustic structure.

Its main open problems are declared in Section 16: the coefficient κ in a0 = κcΓ is not derived, the amount of the Ψ component is taken from data, galaxy clusters require a capture mechanism not yet found, and the baryonic Tully–Fisher slope is in tension with our own fit.
